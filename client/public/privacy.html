Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026 · Starcraft Coach (starcraftcoach.com)

This privacy policy explains how Pinnacle Designs (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Starcraft Coach website and the optional Windows desktop application.

What this service is

Starcraft Coach is a free Direct Strike counter tool for StarCraft II. The website runs counter lookups in your browser. The desktop app adds overlay panels and optional local features while you play.

Information we collect

  • Usage analytics (website). We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use starcraftcoach.com (for example page views and approximate location derived by Google). Google may set cookies or use similar technologies as described in Google’s policies.
  • Advertising (if enabled). If we show ads through Google AdSense or related Google advertising services, Google and partners may use cookies or device identifiers to serve and measure ads according to their policies. You can manage ad personalization in your Google account settings and browser cookie controls.
  • Local app data. The desktop app and browser may store settings, overlay positions, and optional capture/training data on your device. That data stays on your machine unless you choose to share it.
  • Downloads. Installer downloads are served from our download host. Standard server or CDN logs (such as IP address and request time) may be recorded for security and reliability.

What we do not sell

We do not sell your personal information. We do not require an account to use the free web tool.

Third-party services

The site may load resources from Google (Analytics, fonts, and optionally AdSense). Those services process data under Google’s terms and privacy policy. Counter guide links in the footer point to independent third-party sites we do not control.

Children

Starcraft Coach is a general-audience gaming utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact
